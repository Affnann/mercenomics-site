What it is

For seventeen years the United States Department of Defense counted the contractor personnel working for it in the Central Command area, which covers Iraq, Afghanistan, Syria and the surrounding region, and recorded whether they were United States citizens, third-country nationals or local nationals. The reports existed only as 68 separate PDF files, and the series has ended: the April 2025 report states that it is the last.

This archive puts every report into one checked table. At the peak, in 2008, about 266,700 contractor personnel were counted; by 2025, about 20,400. It can be used to compare the use of contractors with troop levels over time, to follow how many workers came from third countries, and to check official statements about the size of the United States presence.

What it cannot be used to say

  • It counts only contractors on Department of Defense contracts in one command's area. Contractors of other United States agencies are not included, and it is not a count of mercenaries.
  • The areas covered change over time. Afghanistan appears until the fourth quarter of the 2021 financial year. Iraq is reported alone until early 2018, then together with Syria. The reports say Israel is included in the totals from the fourth quarter of the 2021 financial year.
  • The first two reports have no separate row for other locations, although their totals include them.
  • The report for the fourth quarter of the 2010 financial year is a recount: a first count of 194,405 was redone and gave 176,340. The dataset uses the recount, as printed.
  • Numbers are kept exactly as printed. Three reports contain tables that do not add up; they are flagged in the file, not corrected.

How it was checked

  • Every table was read twice with two different PDF programs, and both readings agree for all 251 rows.
  • Every row was checked to add up, and every report's areas were checked against its own total.
  • Each total was compared with the figure in the report's own summary text, and each problem found was confirmed against an image of the printed page.

What is in the file

ColumnMeaning
report_fileThe source PDF in data/raw/
fiscal_year, fiscal_quarterThe U.S. fiscal year and quarter the count covers (the fiscal year starts on 1 October)
publishedMonth the report was published, from its file name

label_in_reportThe row label exactly as printed, including footnote marks
totalTotal contractor personnel
us_citizensU.S. citizens
third_country_nationalsCitizens of neither the U.S. nor the host country
local_nationalsLocal or host-country nationals
