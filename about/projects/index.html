In preparation

Working papers

Papers on contracting, principal-agent problems, information asymmetry in recruitment, and where the cost of outsourced military work lands. Each will be published as a preprint with its data and code, so the results can be reproduced. See Research.

Published

Ten datasets

The Department of Defense contractor census, Defense Base Act injury and death claims, US federal guard-service contracts, federal enforcement actions against the largest guard contractors, the private security workforce in five countries, security companies on five official lists, Japan's traffic-guard pay rates, UN peacekeeping contributions, Bangladeshi migration costs, and kidnapping for ransom in the Sundarbans. Each is checked against its source and has a permanent identifier for citing. Download them.

Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the terms these records use, grouped by the actors, the records and the economics. Written because a single misread word in this field turns a figure into a false claim. Read it.

Starting library

The public sources the figures in this field actually come from: the official records, the existing compilations, the academic datasets, and one honest piece on how hard this data is to get right. Every entry opened and checked before it was listed. Read it.

Standing commitments

  • Every dataset: a documentation file naming the sources, collection dates, definitions, known gaps and limits.
  • Every dataset: published under CC BY 4.0, free to download and reuse with credit.
  • Every dataset that is no longer being updated: archived as a versioned file with a permanent identifier.
  • Every correction: posted in the open on the Corrections page.
